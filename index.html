Privacy Policy

DrawTime Kids: Draw & Play
Developer: Sofotive
Last updated: October 15, 2026

About DrawTime Kids

DrawTime Kids: Draw & Play ("DrawTime Kids") is an Android educational drawing and coloring app designed for children. It includes drawing levels, ABC and Numbers activities, Custom Studio, My Art, Sticker Book rewards, Creative Challenges, and optional voice and audio features.

This policy explains how the app handles information and the choices available to parents and guardians.

Information We Collect

During normal drawing and learning activities, Sofotive does not receive personal information, artwork, progress, or usage reports from the app. The app processes drawings, learning progress, and settings on the device to provide its features.

Device speech services and gallery backup services can involve other providers, as explained below. These are different from sending information to Sofotive. External sharing from this version of the app is unavailable.

If you email Sofotive for support or a privacy inquiry, we receive your email address, your message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond to and manage your request. Please avoid including unnecessary information about a child.

Information We Do Not Collect

The app does not ask for a child's name, email address, date of birth, home address, or account credentials. It does not require an account or login.

Sofotive does not use the app to collect location, contacts, microphone recordings, camera images, advertising identifiers, or other persistent device identifiers. The app does not scan your existing photo library, contacts, or messages. It does not send Sofotive behavioral analytics or crash reports.

There are no social profiles, chat, in-app messaging, public artwork uploads, or user-to-user interaction within DrawTime Kids.

Children's Privacy

DrawTime Kids is designed for children. Sofotive does not knowingly collect children's personal information through the app's drawing and learning activities. Those activities do not require children to provide personal information to Sofotive.

Parents and guardians should supervise saving artwork to the device gallery and avoid including a child's name, address, school, or other identifying details in artwork or support messages. If you believe a child has sent personal information to Sofotive, contact sofotive@gmail.com so we can address the concern and any deletion request.

Local App Data

The app stores information on your device so activities can continue between sessions. This includes:

  • Completed drawing levels and the progress used to unlock later levels and stickers.
  • ABC and Numbers tracing and coloring progress.
  • Voice, learning-fact, music, and sound-effect preferences.
  • Older testing versions may have stored an External Sharing preference, a salted parent PIN hash, and failed-attempt/cooldown information. This version does not offer parent PIN setup or external sharing and does not use those old settings to enable sharing. Existing records are not automatically erased.
  • Artwork saved to My Art, including Custom Studio and Creative Challenge creations.

Sofotive does not operate an account-based cloud storage or synchronization service for this data.

Android backup: This version is configured to disable Android app-data backup and exclude app data from supported cloud backup and device-to-device transfer mechanisms. Local progress, settings and My Art should not be relied on to transfer automatically to another device. These settings do not erase existing local data or copies already held by a backup provider. The configured restrictions have not yet been verified in a compiled Android release or on physical devices, so backup and transfer behavior is not guaranteed on every device. Sofotive does not receive device backups. Gallery images are outside these app-data exclusions.

Drawings, Artwork and Saved Content

Drawings are created and processed on the device. Artwork saved inside My Art is stored in the app's local storage, including the drawing or coloring information, its title, and its creation date. Sofotive does not automatically receive or upload artwork.

Saving an image to the device gallery creates a separate image outside the app's private storage. The app does not offer external sharing in this version. Other apps with access to saved gallery images may back up or share them according to their settings.

Device Text-to-Speech and Audio

Voice pronunciation and educational facts use the device's text-to-speech service. The app supplies built-in activity names, letters, numbers, and educational sentences. It does not record a child's voice or send drawings to the speech service.

Speech may be generated locally or may involve an online service, depending on the installed speech engine, selected voice, and device settings. An online speech provider may process the supplied text and connection information under its own privacy practices. We do not guarantee that every device's speech feature works entirely offline.

Sound effects and background music are included with the app and played on the device. Playing these sounds does not itself send information to Sofotive. Voice, learning facts, music, and sound effects can be controlled in Audio Settings.

Save and Share Features

Save: When you choose Save Image or Save Sticker, the app prepares an image on the device and saves it to the device's media library. If Android requires a storage or media-saving permission, the app requests it as part of this action, not simply because you open the app. Permission allows the app to save the image you selected; the app does not use this feature to browse your existing photos.

Share: External sharing is unavailable in this version. There are no Share actions or controls to enable sharing, and the app does not open an external share sheet. Older saved PINs or sharing preferences cannot reactivate it. Local Save, My Art, Sticker Book, and existing artwork and progress remain available.

Saving an image does not open a sharing destination or require a parent PIN. Sofotive does not automatically upload or share your artwork.

You can still use other apps outside DrawTime Kids to access or share gallery images. Those providers' privacy practices apply. If your gallery or photos service automatically backs up new images, saved pictures may be uploaded by that service according to your settings.

Device Permissions

  • Storage/media saving: Used to save images you choose. Whether Android displays a permission prompt depends on its version and your existing permission choices.
  • Local file storage: Used for progress, preferences, saved artwork, and temporary export images.
  • Audio settings: Used to manage playback of the app's sounds.
  • Internet capability: The Android app includes network permission, but its drawing and learning code does not use it to upload artwork, progress, or personal information to Sofotive. Device speech services may require internet access.

The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, or notification permission. It does not request broad access to read your existing photos, videos, or audio library.

Internet and Third-Party Services

Core drawing, coloring, learning content, progress, and saved artwork are intended to work primarily offline. The app has no Sofotive-operated upload server or cloud account service.

Services outside the app can be involved when your device generates speech or backs up gallery images. Other apps can also share images you saved to the gallery. Their availability and handling of information depend on your device, provider, and settings. App-data backup restrictions are described above.

Advertising

DrawTime Kids currently does not display third-party advertising.

Analytics

Sofotive currently does not use third-party analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services in the app. Platform-level diagnostics collected independently by Android or your device provider are governed by that provider's settings and policies.

Data Retention

Local progress, preferences, and saved artwork remain on the device until removed or reset. You can delete individual saved artworks in My Art and reset ABC or Numbers progress through the relevant activity's reset option. Android's Clear storage function or uninstalling the app removes its local app data. This version is configured to restrict app-data backup and transfer as described above; previously created backup copies are not deleted by those restrictions.

Temporary export files are prepared in the app's cache, and the app attempts to remove them when the local Save operation finishes or is cancelled. If a Save operation is interrupted, cached files may remain until cleared by the app's storage controls or operating system.

Gallery images, backup copies, and copies held by recipients or other services must be managed separately. Deleting an artwork in My Art or uninstalling DrawTime Kids does not delete these external copies.

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the inquiry and related follow-up, or as required by law. You may contact us to request its deletion.

Data Security

Progress and artwork stored inside the app use Android's app storage protections. The app does not add a separate encryption layer to its local progress and artwork records. Any retained parent PIN record from an older testing version contains a salted hash rather than the plain PIN. Parent PIN setup and external sharing are unavailable in this version. No parental identity or legally verified parental-consent service is provided. Keeping your device updated and protected with a screen lock helps protect this information.

Sofotive cannot control the security of images after you export them, your device's backup service, or third-party sharing destinations. No storage or communication method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Parental Choices and Controls

Parents and guardians can:

  • Turn voice, learning facts, sound effects, or music on or off in Audio Settings.
  • Use the app with external sharing disabled; it cannot be enabled in this version.
  • Choose whether to save an image to the gallery and supervise its use in other apps.
  • Manage app permissions, speech-engine settings, and backup settings through Android.
  • Delete saved artwork in My Art, reset ABC or Numbers progress, or clear the app's local storage.
  • Contact Sofotive about privacy questions or information sent directly to us.

Sofotive cannot remotely access or delete drawings or progress held only on your device. There is no account to delete. Clearing local storage removes progress and saved artwork from the app, so save any pictures you want to keep first.

External Services and Links

This policy covers DrawTime Kids. It does not govern other apps used to access saved gallery images, device speech engines, backup services, or email providers. Review their privacy policies before using them or providing personal information.

The current app does not include embedded social feeds, a public artwork community, or an in-app web browser.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when the app or its information practices change. The current policy will show its latest revision date. Any new data practices will be reflected in the policy and, where required, explained through additional notices or consent choices.
